<b>Yomimoto</b> (読本) is a Japanese book reader with instant dictionary lookup and Anki card creation, built for e-ink Android devices.<br><br><li> 📚 Read books offline via an <b>embedded ッツ Ebook Reader</b> (EPUB, HTMLZ)</li><li> 🖼️ Read and mine manga pre-processed with <b>Mokuro</b>, and export or crop the current image</li><li> 📖 Tap and drag selection of text for <b>instant dictionary lookups</b></li><li> 📘 <b>Use Yomichan and Migaku dictionaries</b> with pitch and frequency information</li><li> 🃏 <b>Export flashcards to AnkiDroid</b>, complete with image and audio of the current context</li><li> ⛏ <b>Frictionless sentence mining workflow</b> with support for modular enhancements and automations</li><br>Yomimoto is a fork of jidoujisho by Arianne Orpilla.
